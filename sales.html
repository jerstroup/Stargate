// SALES · AGENT SYSTEM

A sales team of agents that turns pipeline into revenue

Seven agents that work like a small revenue team. Two find and qualify the right accounts. One prepares you for every call and coaches you after it. One handles proposals and pricing. One hands won customers to onboarding cleanly. One keeps the CRM and forecast accurate. An orchestrator decides which deals get your time. You keep the relationships, and every message to a buyer is sent by a person. Copy the setup prompt into Claude. It will interview you about your sales motion and then write the whole system as a folder of agent files.

ICP & qualification outbound meeting prep call coaching deal desk handoff & expansion pipeline & forecast

The goal the system works toward

Every agent, every task, and every weekly report is judged against one outcome.

// north star

Turn qualified leads and target accounts into closed revenue predictably: enough pipeline, shorter cycles, higher win rates, and customers handed off cleanly.

The system doesn't count emails sent or meetings booked as wins. Those numbers only matter when deals move forward. Inbound leads come from the Marketing agent system, and the Pipeline agent reports back which lead sources actually close, so Marketing knows where to spend.

Primary
New bookings / month (vs. target)
Pipeline
Qualified pipeline ≥ 3× target
Efficiency
Win rate · sales-cycle length
Guardrail
No auto-sent outreach · forecast within ±10%

How the agents work together

The orchestrator sets the week's account and deal priorities. Work moves from building pipeline to advancing deals and then closing them. Inbound leads arrive from Marketing. Pipeline & Forecast tracks every stage and sends a scorecard back so the next week's plan is based on the deals that actually moved. Click any agent to jump to its role.

The agents and what each one does

Each agent becomes its own markdown file. The defaults below are best practice. The setup interview adjusts them to your deal size, sales motion, team and tools.

// control

Sales Orchestrator

Acts as head of sales and revenue operations. Decides which accounts and deals get your time this week, enforces the sales process, and brings you only the decisions and messages that need you.

Does
  • Runs the weekly pipeline review: what moved, what's stuck, which 3–5 deals matter most, and the next step for each.
  • Chooses a sales method that fits your deal size: SPICED for smaller, faster deals and MEDDICC for enterprise. It also makes sure each deal stage has clear entry and exit criteria.
  • Collects every draft message, proposal and discount into one approval list, and tells Marketing which lead sources close.
Asks you
  • Revenue target, how you sell today (founder-led or a team), typical deal size and cycle length, and how much time you can spend selling each week.
→ agents/sales-orchestrator.md
// 01 · build pipeline

Research & Qualification

Makes sure you only spend time on accounts that fit and have a reason to buy now.

Does
  • Turns your ICP into a scoring rubric: company traits, trigger events and disqualifiers, each with a weight (see the example icp.md).
  • Scores inbound leads from Marketing within minutes and sends good ones on to a meeting.
  • Writes a one-page account brief with a "why this company, why now" angle. If it can't find one, it recommends skipping the account.
Asks you
  • Your best 3–5 customers and why they bought, the deals you'd walk away from, and any data tools you use (Apollo, Clay, Sales Navigator, ZoomInfo).
→ agents/research-qualification.md
// 01 · build pipeline

Prospecting & Outbound

Builds target lists from trigger events and drafts outreach that reads like you wrote it.

Does
  • Drafts one-to-one messages that open with a specific, recent fact about the prospect and end with one small question.
  • Plans sequences of 4–6 touches over 2–3 weeks across email, LinkedIn and phone.
  • Never sends automatically. Every message waits as a draft until a person sends it. It also handles email deliverability and anti-spam rules (CAN-SPAM, GDPR, opt-outs).
Asks you
  • How much outbound you want, who sends, your email or sequencing tool, and which regions you sell into.
→ agents/prospecting-outbound.md
// 02 · advance

Meeting Prep & Call Coach

Makes every call count. You walk in prepared and walk out with a clear next step.

Does
  • Sends a brief 30 minutes before each call: who's attending, the deal's history, 3 goals for the call, the objections likely to come up, and one new fact.
  • After the call: a summary, the qualification details captured for the CRM, and a follow-up email drafted the same day with a dated next step.
  • Weekly coaching: did you quantify the pain, find the economic buyer, and agree a next step with a date?
Asks you
  • Your call recorder (Gong, Fireflies, Granola, Zoom), calendar access, and your discovery questions.
→ agents/meeting-coach.md
// 03 · close

Deal Desk & Proposals

Takes the friction out of closing: fast proposals, consistent pricing, and no surprises in the contract.

Does
  • Drafts proposals, ROI and business cases, and mutual action plans (dated steps both sides agree to) from your templates.
  • Applies pricing guardrails and logs every discount with a reason, so it can spot a pattern such as "we give 30% off every Q4 deal".
  • Summarizes customer redlines and flags non-standard terms for Legal. It drafts security questionnaire answers from approved responses.
Asks you
  • Pricing and packaging, who can approve discounts, contract templates, and your e-signature tool.
→ agents/deal-desk.md
// 03 · close

Customer Handoff & Expansion

Makes the move from sales to onboarding to support feel like one company, and spots chances to grow the account.

Does
  • Within 24 hours of a win, writes a handoff document from the calls and the contract: goals, success criteria, stakeholders, and every promise made.
  • Drafts the kickoff agenda and the 30/60/90-day check-ins, and passes context to Support.
  • Watches for expansion signals (usage, new teams) and flags renewals 90 days ahead. It asks for referrals and case studies once customers are succeeding.
Asks you
  • Who onboards customers, your customer-success or support tool, and your renewal terms.
→ agents/handoff-expansion.md
// measure & learn

Pipeline & Forecast

Keeps the CRM accurate so the forecast holds up. It also does the CRM data entry, so nobody has to type into it.

Does
  • If you have no CRM, recommends one: HubSpot's free CRM as the default, with Attio or Pipedrive as lighter alternatives and Salesforce for enterprise. If you already have one, asks you to connect it.
  • Logs emails, calls and notes automatically, and flags any deal with no activity for 14 days along with a suggested next step.
  • Produces a weekly forecast split into commit, best case and pipeline, plus a scorecard: pipeline coverage, win rate, cycle length, and which lead sources close.
Asks you
  • Your CRM, your current pipeline and win rate if known, and whether it may write to the CRM (after an approved read-only period).
→ agents/pipeline-forecast.md

Never let agents send outbound on their own. Automated, AI-written outreach is damaging the reputation of the startups that use it, and the email domains they send from. In this system agents research and draft, and a person reads and sends every message to a buyer.

// always needs your approval

  • Any message, email or sequence step sent to a prospect or customer
  • Proposals, pricing, discounts and quotes
  • Contract terms, redlines and security answers
  • Commitments to customers (features, timelines, SLAs)
  • Buying contact data or bulk-changing the CRM

// red flags to watch

  • Lots of first meetings but few second ones: qualification is too loose
  • Deals stuck in one stage: no champion or economic buyer
  • Average discount creeping up: weak value case or no pricing guardrails
  • Reply rates falling: too much automation or a deliverability problem
  • Forecast misses: stage exit criteria aren't enforced
